1. Introduction

The Centre for Blockchain and Intelligent Technology (CBIT) at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website at cbit.cuhk.edu.cn.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, browser type, device information, and referring URLs collected through standard web server logs.
  • AI Chat Interactions: Questions you submit to our AI assistant are processed to generate responses. These interactions may be logged for quality improvement.
  • Voluntary Information: Information you provide when contacting us via email or submitting forms, such as your name, email address, and affiliation.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our website and services
  • To improve our AI assistant's response quality
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide support
  • To analyze website usage patterns and improve user experience
  • To send conference-related notifications if you have opted in

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with:

  • The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, as our parent institution
  • Service providers who assist in website hosting and maintenance
  • As required by law or to protect our rights

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies for essential functionality such as language preferences and theme settings. These are stored locally in your browser and are not transmitted to external servers.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (e.g., university websites, conference pages, GitHub). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of non-essential data collection
