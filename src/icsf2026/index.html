会议简介

Introduction

智慧金融国际会议（ICSF）创办于2016年，并隶属于信息系统协会（AIS）。多年来，ICSF 先后在香港城市大学（深圳）、中山大学、北京工业大学、香港中文大学（深圳）、香港大学、卧龙岗大学（迪拜）、中国科学技术大学、同济大学举办，推动研究人员与行业领袖之间围绕前沿议题展开深入交流与对话。

智慧金融将金融科技与人工智能、区块链、大数据等数据驱动技术相结合，并融合数字货币、智能合约等新型金融模式。随着基础模型的快速演进，它们正推动智能金融的变革与升级，进一步拓展金融创新的边界。这些技术通过预测分析、个性化服务与风险管理等方式提升金融系统效能，但同时也带来伦理、隐私与监管等方面的挑战。

ICSF2026 将通过主题演讲与论文分会等环节，搭建学术研究与产业实践之间的桥梁，聚焦并回应相关挑战，挖掘人工智能驱动金融创新和金融监管的机遇。

The International Conference on Smart Finance (ICSF) was founded in 2016 and is affiliated with the Association for Information Systems (AIS). Over the years, ICSF has been hosted by City University of Hong Kong (Shenzhen), Sun Yat-sen University, Beijing University of Technology, The Chinese University of Hong Kong (Shenzhen), The University of Hong Kong, the University of Wollongong (Dubai), the University of Science and Technology of China, and Tongji University, fostering in-depth exchanges and dialogue between researchers and industry leaders on cutting-edge topics.

Smart finance integrates financial technology with data-driven technologies such as artificial intelligence, blockchain, and big data, while also incorporating emerging financial models including digital currencies and smart contracts. Through keynote speeches and paper sessions, ICSF 2026 will build a bridge between academic research and industry practice, focusing on and addressing these challenges while exploring opportunities for AI-driven financial innovation and market regulation.
